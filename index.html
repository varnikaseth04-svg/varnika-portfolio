About Varnika Seth

I am a graphic designer and visual artist combining a structured business administration foundation with hands-on visual storytelling. My practice centers on translating raw concepts into coherent visual frameworks—from editorial slide decks and luxury brand packaging to digital animation.

Whether calculating typography scale for a physical die-cut box or keyframing vector assets, I prioritize precision, identity harmony, and clean presentation.
